How to Play

Notes travel from the center toward 8 pads. Tap the matching pad as the note reaches it.

Change Visual to switch between Heart, Diamond, Club and Spade themes.

On PC use the spatial keys:

Q   W   E
A      D
Z   X   C

Mouse clicks also work. Space pauses.
